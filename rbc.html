THE QUESTION

How do earnings performance, credit quality, and valuation assumptions shape the investment discussion?

The research examines FY2025 reported results, Q3 FY2026 interim data, and FY2026–FY2027 consensus estimates. Reported results, vendor estimates, and author-selected assumptions are identified separately.

MY APPROACH

From question to evidence.

Reconcile performance

Build a revenue-to-net-income bridge and reconcile segment contributions to consolidated results, preserving period and accounting comparability.

Assess quality and context

Review profitability, efficiency, capital and credit measures alongside selected peer multiples and historical price-return relationships.

Make assumptions visible

Construct bear, base, and bull scenarios using explicit EPS, P/E, and currency assumptions. Package the research into a report and a concise carousel using Python.

RESEARCH DISCIPLINE

A conclusion is only as useful
as its assumptions.

Comparable periods

Preserve accounting definitions and distinguish annual results from interim ratios.

Explicit currency

Keep CAD reporting separate from USD share-price comparisons and state conversion assumptions.

Traceable evidence

Provide source notes, calculation methods, structured CSV data, and the presentation generator.

WHAT THE WORK SHOWS

Findings, with context.

Start with reconciliation

The study reconciles changes in revenue, provisions, expenses, and taxes to explain the change in reported net income.

Separate fact from assumption

Reported accounting results are distinguished from consensus forecasts and author-chosen valuation inputs.

Communicate sensitivity

The scenario model shows how earnings, valuation multiples, and exchange-rate assumptions affect the implied price; scenarios are not probabilities.

Scope & limitations

Independent educational research, not employment at or work commissioned by Royal Bank of Canada. Findings reflect the 5 November 2026 cutoff and are not live quotes. Valuation scenarios are illustrative and are not individualized investment advice.

Figures and methods are drawn from the linked project documentation and recorded outputs.
